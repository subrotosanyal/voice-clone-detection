Why this mark

Every part of it means something

Nothing here is decorative — each choice maps to a real part of how Satya-Vani actually works.

The calm waveform

Five bars, rising and falling like a natural voice's amplitude — the shape of ordinary, unremarkable human speech. This is what the system stays silent about.

The break

Two bars stop following the pattern and turn red — the exact moment a waveform stops behaving like a human throat and starts behaving like a machine.

The open ring

Most trust marks close into a reassuring circle. This one doesn't — the gap sits exactly where the waveform breaks, because finding that gap is the entire product.
